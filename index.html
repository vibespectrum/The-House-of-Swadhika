About Us — Home Made With Love

Run by Mom & Dad — a family kitchen rooted in tradition.

The name Swadhika comes from the Sanskrit roots Swa (self) and Adhika (growth, empowerment) — true empowerment starts within, through mindful living, honest work, and food made with integrity.

The House of Swadhika is rooted in traditional cooking, where food is prepared the way it has always been — slow, mindful, and with respect. Handcrafted, and made with quality ingredients.

We cook only with fresh rice bran oil & ghee, homemade ginger-garlic paste, and home-ground spices & masala — while avoiding palm oil, preservatives, artificial colours, chemicals, and all unnatural ingredients. What leaves our kitchen is homemade food, prepared with care and love every single time.
